### ⚙️ How to Install (Sideload Guide)
Since this APK is distributed directly outside the Google Play Store, your device might show an "Unknown Sources" warning. Follow these simple steps to install:
1. Click the download link above and download the `.apk` file.
2. Open the downloaded file. If a security prompt appears, click **Settings**.
3. Toggle on **"Allow from this source"** or **"Unknown Sources"**.
4. Go back and tap **Install**. Enjoy your music!

## ✨ Key Features

*   🤖 **Gemini-Powered AI DJ:** Get personalized smart playlist mixes tailored to your mood via Firebase AI Logic (Sign in with Google, no personal API key required!).
*   🎶 **Multi-Format Playback:** Seamlessly plays MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV, AAC, and OGG.
*   🎛️ **Graphic Equalizer:** Custom audio shaping with built-in Bass Boost.
*   🌐 **World Radio Integration:** Stream online radio stations globally using the integrated Radio Browser directory with custom country filtering.
*   ☁️ **Cloud Streaming:** Connect and stream your own personal music library straight from the cloud.
*   🎤 **Lyrics & Artwork:** Fullscreen synchronized lyrics display with automatic iTunes artwork fallback.

---

## 📱 Requirements

*   **OS Version:** Android 9.0 or higher.
*   **Permissions:** Storage/Media Access (to scan your local audio files) and Internet Access (for AI DJ & Radio features).
